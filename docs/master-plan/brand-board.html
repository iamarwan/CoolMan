Name

A fresh look does not need a new word. "Sabil" is short, said the same way in both languages, and already means the thing the app maps. It also means "a way" or "a path", which leaves room to grow beyond water. Alternatives are below in case you still want to change it.

Three marks

Simple shapes that still read at the size of a phone icon. These are directions drawn in code; a logo designer should redraw the chosen one and tune the lettering.

The arch recommended

sabilسبيل

The arched window of Cairo's historic sabils, where water was handed out for free, with a drop inside. Only this subject has this shape.

The drop pin

sabilسبيل

A map pin with a drop cut out of it: "water, here". Instantly clear. Many water and map apps use something similar.

The jar

sabilسبيل

The clay water jar left out for passers-by. Goes with direction B. Charming, but it points to the past more than to an app.

One lettering idea for whichever mark you choose: in سبيل the dot under the ب becomes a water drop, and in "sabil" the dot on the i does the same. One small detail that works in both scripts.
