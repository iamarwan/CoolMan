# Nwasy expansion plan

Role: head of expansion at a mobility company.
Date: 2026-11-11.

## The rule: density before distance

A map with ten places in each of twenty districts is useless everywhere. A map
with three hundred places in one district is useful every day. Mobility apps
grow the same way: one zone works, then the next.

So Nwasy opens **one district at a time**, and a district is not "open" until
it passes its numbers.

## What "open" means

| Number | Minimum for a district to count as open |
|---|---|
| Coolers mapped | 150, or the district's count if smaller |
| Confirmed in the last 7 days | 60% of them |
| Monthly users in the district | 1,000 |
| People who confirmed something this month | 100 |
| A local lead (a person who walks it weekly) | 1 |

Do not start the next district until the current one has held these numbers
for four weeks.

## Order of districts

Chosen by: people on foot, heat exposure, giving culture, and distance from
the founder (near first).

| Wave | When | Districts | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Months 1 to 5 | **Maadi** (Degla, Sarayat, Road 9 area) | Test data and the founder's knowledge are here; mixed foot traffic; active neighbourhood groups |
| 2 | Months 9 to 12 | **Downtown and Sayeda Zeinab**; **Nasr City** | The densest foot traffic and many old sabils; a large student and worker population |
| 3 | Year 2, first half | Heliopolis, Mohandessin and Dokki, Shubra, Giza around the university | Fills in Greater Cairo |
| 4 | Year 2, second half | **Alexandria** (first city outside Cairo) | Second-largest city; a real test of running without the founder present |
| 5 | Year 3 | Mansoura, Tanta, Assiut; then a first city abroad | Only if Alexandria ran on its playbook |

Waves 3 to 5 are a direction, not a commitment. Each is decided by the
numbers of the wave before.

## District launch checklist

**Four weeks before**
- Pick the district lead. Pay per verified cooler added in the first month,
  then a small monthly amount for a weekly walk.
- Join the district's Facebook and WhatsApp groups; introduce the project.
- List mosques, markets, transport stops, schools: that is where coolers are.

**Two weeks before**
- The lead and two helpers walk every street and add every cooler with a
  photo. Target: 80% of the district's coolers before any announcement.
- Note every food truck and cart seen, for layer 2.
- Record three corner portraits for launch week.

**Launch week**
- Post "we opened in…" with the count and a map picture.
- Stickers on the most visible coolers, with hosts' permission.
- One "thirsty test" video in the district.
- Approach three mosques and three shops that host coolers about care plans.

**Weeks 2 to 8**
- Weekly: check the numbers; walk and fix streets with stale coolers.
- Offer free vendor listings; hand out vendor stickers.
- First sabil enquiries from the district are answered within a day.

**Decision at week 8**
- Numbers met and held: the district is open; the lead continues.
- Not met: find out why before spending anything on another district.

## Cost to open a district (*assumptions*)

| Item | EGP |
|---|---|
| Mapping: 200 coolers at 15 each | 3,000 |
| Lead for two months | 6,000 |
| Stickers and printing | 2,500 |
| Local adverts in groups and on social | 6,000 |
| Transport and filming | 2,500 |
| **Total** | **20,000** |

A district pays this back with four sabil sales. That is the number to quote
when asked what expansion money buys: about EGP 20,000 and eight weeks per
district.

## Who runs it

| Stage | Structure |
|---|---|
| District 1 | The founder is the lead. |
| Districts 2 to 4 | One paid lead each; the founder checks numbers weekly. |
| From district 5 | An operations coordinator manages leads and the technician routes. |
| Another city | A city manager on salary plus a share of that city's sales. |

## Technician routes

Coolers under care in one district are visited on one route on one day. The
care cost of 6,000 a year assumes about ten coolers per technician day.
Selling care plans to scattered single coolers in far districts breaks that
number, so care plans are sold only in open districts.

## Outside Egypt

The need exists across hot cities in the region, and the word "sabil" is
understood from Morocco to the Gulf. Conditions before any country:

- Three Egyptian cities run by local teams without the founder.
- Vendor plans paying in at least five districts.
- A local partner who brings the first 300 mapped places.

First candidates, in order: Jeddah or Riyadh (strong giving culture, high
heat, high smartphone use), Amman, Casablanca. This is year 3 at the earliest
and belongs in the pitch as direction only.

## Risks specific to expansion

| Risk | Sign | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Opening too many districts | Confirmed-in-7-days falls below 50% anywhere | Freeze new districts; send the walk team back |
| Leads adding fake places for pay | Many places with no later confirmations | Pay only after two different users confirm a place |
| Hosts object to stickers or photos | Complaints | Always ask first; remove on request the same day |
| Local authorities question the mapping | A call or visit | Carry a one-page letter explaining the project; involve the district office early |
