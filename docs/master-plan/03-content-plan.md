# Nwasy content plan

Role: creative director running Egyptian social accounts.
Date: 2026-11-11. Covers the first 90 days after the public launch.

The Arabic lines here are drafts in Egyptian dialect. Have a native copywriter
read them aloud before posting; I can write Arabic but cannot hear it.

## The one idea

**"على الناصية" (on the corner).** Everything good about a neighbourhood is on
its corners: the cold water someone left for strangers, the coffee cart, the
man who fixes your tyre. Nwasy is the map of that. The content shows real
corners and the people behind them.

Three rules:
1. **Real street, real people.** No stock footage.
2. **One person can make it** with a phone, in under an hour.
3. **Show the app for three seconds, not thirty.** The story is the corner.

## Who we talk to

| Audience | Where they are | What they care about |
|---|---|---|
| People on foot: students, workers, riders | TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook | Free cold water now, good cheap food and coffee nearby |
| Families who give | Facebook, WhatsApp groups | Doing something lasting for a parent; being sure it works |
| Street vendors | Facebook, WhatsApp, in person | More customers; respect for their work |
| Neighbourhood groups | Facebook groups (Maadi has large ones) | Their area, pride, safety |

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | What it is | Example hook (Arabic, then meaning) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. The corner** | 40% | A 20 to 40 second portrait of one cooler or one vendor and the person behind it | "الكولدير ده بقاله ١٢ سنة.. تعرف مين اللي حاطه؟" (This cooler has been here 12 years. Know who put it there?) |
| **2. Thirsty test** | 20% | Founder walks an area at noon and finds water using only the app | "الساعة ١٢ الضهر في المعادي.. هلاقي مَيّة ساقعة في كام دقيقة؟" (Noon in Maadi. How many minutes to find cold water?) |
| **3. Their name** | 15% | A family's sabil: the dedication, the first person drinking, the page showing confirmations | "عملوا سبيل باسم والدهم.. وبيشوفوه شغّال كل يوم" (They built a sabil in their father's name, and see it working every day.) |
| **4. Map facts** | 15% | One number from the map as a simple graphic | "في المعادي ٢١٤ كولدير.. ٣١ منهم مش شغّالين النهارده" (Maadi has 214 coolers; 31 are not working today.) |
| **5. How to** | 10% | 15-second screen recordings | "ضيف كولدير في ٣٠ ثانية" (Add a cooler in 30 seconds.) |

Numbers in pillar 4 must come from the real map on the day of posting.

## Formats one person can make

| Format | Length | Shot list |
|---|---|---|
| Corner portrait | 20 to 40 s | Wide shot of the corner; hands at the tap or the coffee; the person says one sentence; 3 seconds of the pin in the app; end card |
| Thirsty test | 30 to 45 s | Selfie start with the time and temperature; walk with the phone; arrive; drink; tap "Still working" |
| Fact card | Still image | One number, one line, the brand mark. Made from a template. |
| Screen tip | 15 s | Screen recording with one caption |

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Post | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | Corner portrait | TikTok, Reels, Facebook |
| Monday | Fact card | Facebook, Instagram, neighbourhood groups |
| Tuesday | Screen tip | Stories, TikTok |
| Wednesday | Thirsty test | TikTok, Reels |
| Thursday | Corner portrait (a vendor) | TikTok, Reels, Facebook |
| Friday | Their name, or a thank-you to the week's top contributors | Facebook, WhatsApp status |
| Saturday | Off; answer comments | |

Five posts a week. Film two portraits and one thirsty test in a single
half-day walk.

## First 30 days, post by post

| Week | Posts |
|---|---|
| 1 | Launch film: "على الناصية" (60 s, six corners, no narration until the last line). Fact card: how many coolers are mapped. Screen tip: find the nearest cooler. Thirsty test 1. Portrait: the oldest cooler found. |
| 2 | Portrait: a mosque caretaker who cleans a cooler daily. Fact card: how many are not working today. Screen tip: confirm in one tap, and why you must be there. Thirsty test 2 (a rider does it). Portrait: a coffee cart. |
| 3 | Their name 1 (first family, with permission). Fact card: top five streets for water. Screen tip: add a cooler. Thirsty test 3 (different district, to tease expansion). Portrait: a juice stand. |
| 4 | Thank-you: the ten top contributors by name. Fact card: confirmations this month. Screen tip: Arabic and English. Thirsty test 4. Portrait: a family's sabil being installed. |

## Seasonal campaigns

| When | Campaign | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Ramadan | "سبيل في رمضان" | Daily portrait before iftar; the free-iftar-tables layer; families' dedications |
| May to September | "صيف من غير عطش" (a summer without thirst) | Thirsty tests in the hottest hours; partner with a riders' group; cooler count race between districts |
| Back to school | "ناصية المدرسة" | Coolers and carts near schools and universities |
| Each new district | "افتحنا في…" (we opened in…) | The first 100 coolers there, mapped by named locals |

## Street presence

- **Plaque** on every sabil we build: dedication, "اشرب وادعيلهم" (drink, and
  say a prayer for them), QR code to its page.
- **Sticker** for any cooler on the map: "الكولدير ده على نواصي" with a QR
  code. Printed cheaply, placed with the host's permission.
- **Vendor sticker** for carts: "لاقيني على نواصي" (find me on Nwasy).

Each QR code opens that place in the app, or the store page if the app is not
installed (needs share links, see the roadmap).

## Voice

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Egyptian dialect in Arabic, plain English | Formal Arabic in social posts |
| Name real streets and people | Vague "communities" |
| Let givers and vendors speak | Preach, or use guilt |
| Mention reward (ثواب) only in the giver's own words | Use religious language as a sales line |
| Show a broken cooler honestly | Hide what is not working |

## What to measure each week

| Number | Target by day 90 |
|---|---|
| App installs from links in posts | 3,000 |
| Monthly active users in the launch district | 1,000 |
| New coolers added by users (not by the founder) | 100 |
| Sabil enquiries from content | 30 |
| Vendors asking to be listed | 50 |
| Best format by installs per post | Known, and doubled down on |

Stop any pillar that produces no installs after eight posts.

## Before filming

- Written or recorded consent from anyone whose face or voice appears, and
  from a family before showing a dedication.
- Never film children's faces.
- Ask the host before filming at a mosque or shop.
