Who we are

Nwasy is a free Android app that maps the useful things on Egyptian street corners. It starts with public drinking-water coolers: where they are, whether they work today, and whether there are cups. People nearby keep it accurate by confirming a cooler from the spot; the app only accepts a confirmation when the phone is within 50 metres.

Next, the same map will show food trucks, coffee and tea carts, juice stands and other street essentials. The logo has to grow with that. It must not read as a water brand.

We also build and maintain coolers that families fund as sadaqa jariya (ongoing charity), often in memory of a parent. Each one carries a plaque with our mark.

The name

Arabic (fixed)

نواصي means "street corners". Egyptians say على الناصية ("on the corner") for the kiosk, the cart and the cooler.

Latin (not final)

Working spelling is Nwasy. It may change to Nawasy. Said "na-WAA-see". Please build the Latin wordmark so the spelling can change without redrawing the mark.

Who will see it

  • People on foot in the heat: delivery riders, workers, students, commuters. Mid-range Android phones, often in bright sun.
  • Families who fund a cooler: they see the mark on the plaque and on the cooler's page in the app. It must feel trustworthy and respectful.
  • Street vendors: they will put our sticker on their cart. It must feel friendly and local, not corporate.

What the logo should say

"It's here, on this corner, and someone checked." A place, marked and confirmed.

Personality: helpful, street-smart, warm, honest, quick. Think of a neighbour who knows the area, not a charity, a bank or a tech giant.

Must have

  • A symbol that works alone, plus wordmarks in Arabic and Latin.
  • Lockups: horizontal, stacked, Arabic only, Latin only, symbol only. In the bilingual lockup both names carry equal weight.
  • An Arabic wordmark that is drawn or carefully adapted, not a default font typed out. The Latin wordmark should feel like the same family.
  • Works in one colour, on light and dark backgrounds, and in reverse.
  • Stays clear at 24 px.
  • Room to grow: the same mark has to make sense on a coffee cart's sticker.

Please avoid

  • A water drop as the main idea. Every water company uses one, and we are becoming more than water.
  • Religious symbols (crescents, domes, minarets). The charity side is respected, but the brand is for everyone.
  • A plain map pin that looks like Google Maps or any delivery app.
  • A plus or cross shape that reads as medical.
  • Gradients, glows or thin lines that fail in print, engraving or sunlight.
  • Anything that looks like talabat, Careem, Rabbit or other Egyptian apps.
  • AI-generated or stock elements in the final files: we need to register the mark as a trademark.

Starting ideas (optional)

These are prompts to react to, not instructions. Bring your own directions too.

The noon pin

The Arabic letter ن (the first letter of نواصي) is a bowl with a dot above it. Turn the dot into a location marker: "a place, marked".

The corner

Two streets meeting at a right angle, with something placed on the corner.

The confirmed spot

A mark that suggests "checked here": a tick, a stamp or a ring, joined with a place.

Colour and type

Colour is open. The brand palette is being redesigned, so please present round 1 in black and white only. In round 2, propose one or two palettes.

The app already uses fixed status colours: green (working), amber (empty), red (broken), blue (waiting for review) and purple (sponsored). The brand colour must be clearly different from all five, especially the green, and must stay readable in strong sunlight.

The app's text font is IBM Plex Sans Arabic. The wordmarks should sit comfortably next to it.

Process and timing

  1. Round 1 (1 week): three directions in black and white, each shown as app icon, map pin, plaque and bilingual lockup.
  2. Round 2 (1 week): one direction refined, with one or two colour palettes.
  3. Final (1 week): one more round of small changes, then all files.

Show every concept at its real small size (48 px and 24 px), not only large.

Rights

On final payment, all rights to the logo pass to Nwasy, including the right to register it as a trademark. The work must be original. Tell us about any font used in the wordmark and its licence.

What we will send you

  • Screens of the current app, in English and Arabic.
  • Photos of real street coolers, plaques and coffee carts in Cairo.
  • Answers to any questions within one working day.
